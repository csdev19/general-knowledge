We use session and preference mechanisms to make the website and app work. Not every storage technology is a cookie.

## Session and security

The website uses authentication and session-cache cookies to maintain and protect access. Lifetimes depend on the session and authentication mechanism; session caching is configured for 10 minutes. Blocking these cookies may prevent account access. The desktop app stores session credentials locally through its storage mechanisms.

## Language and appearance

The language cookie retains your preference for up to one year. The website stores theme selection in browser local storage until you change it or clear site data. These preferences are not used for advertising.

## Diagnostic technologies

Desktop versions with {{DIAGNOSTICS_PROVIDER}} enabled may store identifiers and diagnostic state locally and send technical events. This is separate from a cookie needed to sign in. The privacy policy explains what is sent. If a new technology or purpose requires consent, it must be requested before activation; these terms do not replace that permission.

## How to manage them

You may clear or block cookies and storage in your browser and manage capture permissions in your operating system. Clearing cookies may sign you out or reset preferences, but does not delete your account or Cloud files. Email the contact address for privacy or deletion requests.
