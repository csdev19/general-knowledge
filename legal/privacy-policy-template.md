## Content on your device

Local recordings, screenshots, projects and exports are stored on your device in locations used or configured in the app. Signing in alone does not upload them to Cloud. When you use an upload feature, the selected file and data needed to manage it are sent to the service. Screen, microphone and camera permissions support the features you activate and are managed through your operating system.

## Account and service data

We process name, email, verification status, account and session identifiers, dates and credentials protected with hashing. Sessions may include IP address and browser or device information. Cloud processes uploaded files, names, type, size, duration where applicable, dates, revisions, status, quota usage and sharing settings. We also process information you send when requesting support.

## How we use data

We use necessary data to create and secure accounts, authenticate you, host and deliver files, manage quotas, answer questions, detect abuse and comply with legal obligations. Processing relies on the requested service, applicable obligations or consent where required. Optional purposes requiring consent must be authorized separately, and you may withdraw consent without retroactive effect. We do not sell personal data, use your recordings for advertising or train AI models on them.

## App diagnostics

When telemetry is enabled in a distribution, {{DIAGNOSTICS_PROVIDER}} receives an installation identifier, technical events, device or version information and error reports with messages, stack traces and context that may include file paths or names. {{PRODUCT_NAME}} disables {{DIAGNOSTICS_PROVIDER}} session recording, automatic click capture and automatic pageviews. This does not mean no technical events or error reports are sent. Diagnostics are not designed to send video or audio from your recordings. You may contact us to exercise rights over this data.

## Sharing and access

When you enable or use a sharing feature, people with access to the link may view or download content according to that feature. Links can be forwarded and cannot recall copies already downloaded. Authorized personnel and providers may access data as needed for operation, support, security or legal obligations; access is limited to its purpose. We do not describe Cloud as end-to-end encrypted storage. We may disclose information for valid legal requests or to address incidents and protect rights, within legal limits.

## Retention and deletion

We retain data as needed for the account, requested features and purposes described here. You may delete files using available features and request account closure by email. Visible deletion may precede technical purging: backups, security logs or legally required records may remain for as long as necessary for their purpose with limited access. We do not promise instant deletion of every copy. When handling a request, we will explain applicable exceptions or timeframes. Cloud closure and quota changes follow the Cloud terms; they do not themselves delete local files.

## Your rights

You may request information, access, correction, updating, deletion or objection to processing, and other applicable rights including portability where relevant. Email us from your account address with your request; we may ask for proportionate information to verify identity. We will respond within legal deadlines and explain limitations. You may approach the competent data protection authority without waiving other remedies.

## Security and changes

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data, but no system eliminates every risk. Do not share passwords or upload information you are not authorized to process. We will update this policy when practices change, showing the date and version and communicating material changes. Publishing an update does not replace consent where required or authorize incompatible new uses of your data.
