# Never

What the owner does not want, each with a one-line why. A rejection in a session is proposed here (or
as a "when not" on an existing entry) and written only after the owner confirms.

## The playful register outside editors and sandboxes

On a dashboard, landing, CLI or settings screen. Why: the owner wants calm everywhere except things made
to tinker with. See [playful-tools.md](./playful-tools.md).

## Taglines, helper text and secondary labels that a layout should carry

Why: if the screen needs them to make sense, the layout is wrong. Exceptions are in the words rule in
[principles.md](./principles.md).

## Copied screenshots in this hub

Of a referenced product, Awwwards or Mobbin. Why: the hub is public and those images belong to their
owners; link and write a note instead.

## Not written yet

The rest of the list is filled the first time the owner rejects something, by proposing an entry and
asking.
