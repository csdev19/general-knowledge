### Highlight the key phrase with the page's headline marker
- **When:** a short text on a landing page (a banner, a notice, a one-line statement) has one phrase that carries its point. Reuse the marker treatment the page's headline uses: on wachiman.dev, the yellow highlighter behind "anywhere".
- **When not:** long paragraphs, or more than one highlighted phrase per block. Never use bold or a new colour as a substitute.
- **Reference:** [wachiman.dev](https://wachiman.dev) hero ("Review anywhere.") and its cookie banner ("one cookie"). Owner's words, translated: "a highlight, like a highlighter pen, like the title."
